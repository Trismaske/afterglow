13. **The pinned-height policy, first cut (deferred decision, built on a default).** The walk's evidence at 1.3× on the S10e (360 dp) and the S23: free text wraps as designed and needs no lever; what breaks is every side-by-side pairing of a fixed-size element with a text column — Home's goal ring beside its numbers (three-line wraps), Stats' Today ring, the Settings pill rows whose title had a fixed 118 dp width (cut) — and the Home brand row, where "Afterglow" broke mid-word beside its three action buttons. Policy as built: (a) text scales freely at every step, no `maxFontSizeMultiplier` anywhere; (b) a side-by-side layout STACKS from the large-text threshold (`lib/textScale.ts`, 1.3 — the first scale the walk saw them break; 1.15 fit), read through `useLargeText`; (c) a row of a title and actions WRAPS its actions under the title rather than breaking the title; (d) fixed-height surfaces that are exact-geometry landings (the UnitCard, the tab bar, the stage boxes) keep their geometry and ellipsize, pending the walk's evidence at 1.5–2.0. Tier: measured for (b)'s threshold; judged for the rest; the close-out grilling decides.
14. **The large-text threshold is relative to the width.** The S23 (411 dp) still held Home's goal row at 1.5× while the S10e (360 dp) lost it at 1.3×; the measure that predicts both is fontScale × 360 / widthDp ≥ 1.3 (`effectiveTextScale`). A wider phone stacks later; a narrower one earlier. Tier: measured on two widths.
15. **Exact-geometry surfaces grow by the font scale, deterministically.** The unit card's header row clipped its title mid-glyph at 1.5× (a 20 dp pinned row); the lever keeps the Timeline's exact `getItemLayout` by making the card height a FUNCTION of the font scale (`unitCardHeight(fontScale)`: the header line scales, the thumbnail strip does not) rather than a constant — the landing stays exact because the height is still computed, never measured. The histogram's two axis rows take the same treatment. Tier: derived; the gate's Timeline landings verify it.
