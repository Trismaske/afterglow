### P9#1. Continue to the lookahead target when whole-groups is enabled

Item B currently states both:

1. page up to `cap + 200` unreviewed photos; and
2. keep chaining slices “until the base cap fills or end of scope.”

The second stopping condition can undo P7#1/P8#2 at a slice boundary.
Suppose the last item in one work slice is unreviewed photo `cap`, and the
first item in the next slice is its similar partner at `cap + 1`. Stopping
when the base cap fills never loads the partner, so component closure
cannot include it.

Pin the continuation targets separately:

- **whole-groups on:** keep chaining bounded slices until `cap + 200`
  unreviewed candidates are collected or end of scope is reached;
- **whole-groups off:** stop at the base `cap` or end of scope, because the
  lookahead would be discarded;
- a slice budget controls yielding/progress/cancellation only—it is not a
  semantic candidate boundary; and
- end of scope with `1..cap-1` unreviewed photos produces the smaller valid
  draw, while end of scope with zero proves the staged-only case.

This preserves all existing limits: each work slice stays bounded,
retention stays at 700, cancellation never promotes a partial candidate,
and a sparse range may take several slices with visible progress.

Add one fixture where the base cap is reached by the final row of a slice
and a similar `cap + 1` row begins the next slice. Assert:

- whole-groups on resumes and includes the partner through component
  closure;
- whole-groups off stops at the cap;
- cancellation between those slices creates/replaces no session; and
- restart resumes or safely restarts analysis without promoting the
  base-only partial candidate.

The existing P8#1 fixture finds the **first** unreviewed photo beyond a
budget. This additional fixture covers the distinct case where forward
progress has been satisfied but similarity lookahead has not.

## Revision-10 verification

Revision 10 otherwise closes the eighth review.

### P8#1 — resolved, subject only to P9#1's lookahead target

- Page-budget exhaustion is a continuation state rather than a draw
  boundary.
- Candidate/reservoir state and the MediaStore cursor survive bounded work
  slices.
- Partial candidates never create or replace a session.
- Cancellation, restart, both orders, and staged-only end-of-scope proof
  are pinned.
- The anti-join alternative carries appropriate proof obligations.

### P8#2 — resolved

- Whole-groups selection is the component closure of every retained
  component intersecting the base set, not only the cut component.
- Closure stays inside the retained 700-photo window.
- Staged-context membership is evaluated against the closed set.
- Non-cut, multiple-component, staged-member, newest-first, and hard-edge
  fixtures assert exact membership.

### P8#3 — resolved

- Interrupted `launching` + authoritative absence records
  `absent_after_interrupted_launch`.
- It repairs current state and queues but earns no reclaimed-byte or
  session credit.
- Present releases the reservation; unknown remains retryable.
- History/UI cannot claim Afterglow performed an ambiguous trash.
- Pre/post-dispatch death is crossed with absent/present/unknown outcomes
  and repeated restart.

### P8#4 — resolved

- `photos.trash_generation` and `trash_reservations.photo_id PRIMARY KEY`
  make generation and one-live-attempt semantics explicit.
- Member rows capture the generation and measured bytes.
- Absence-terminal uniqueness permits cancelled/error retries while
  preventing duplicate completion of one generation.
- Restore increments once; a verified re-trash may count the next
  generation.
- Mixed batch display state derives from member outcomes.
- Identity merge, repeated restore notification, overlapping attempts, and
  real-index inspection are pinned.
- Done-when copy now says one `(photo, trash generation)` is never
  double-counted.

## Implementation watchlist — no additional plan changes required

These points are already implied by revision 10 and its tests; they should
be checked during gate implementation rather than trigger another plan
revision:

- startup reconciliation should treat an interrupted `preparing` batch as
  not dispatched, then either release it or safely resume preparation;
- `trash_reservations` should have explicit FKs/indexes to its batch,
  member, and photo parents with tested deletion order;
- stored batch display state, if retained as a cache, must be updated in
  the same transaction as member outcomes; and
- a resumed candidate scan should deduplicate the boundary row if an OEM
  MediaStore cursor repeats it.
